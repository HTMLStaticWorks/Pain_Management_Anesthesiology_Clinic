Meet Our Doctors

ApexPain was founded by fellowship-trained pain management physicians and anesthesiologists committed to sub-specialty interventional care, fluoroscopic injection precision, and patient reassurance.

Book Physician Consult

Our Mission

To eliminate chronic pain through targeted image-guided interventions, compassionate anesthesiology, and non-opioid multidisciplinary therapies that return patients to active, full lives.

Our Vision

To set the standard in interventional pain medicine and outpatient anesthesiology by delivering sub-millimeter injection accuracy and personalized patient outcome tracking.

Clinical Excellence Pillars

The principles guiding our physicians, anesthesiologists, and nursing team.

AAAHC Safety Standards

State-of-the-art accredited outpatient surgical suites with real-time fluoroscopic C-arm imaging and sterile protocol.

Sub-Specialty Anesthesia

Attending anesthesiologists providing monitored intravenous sedation for painless, reassuring outpatient procedures.

Neuromodulation

Pioneering spinal cord stimulation (SCS) trials and radiofrequency neurotomy for complex neuropathic pain conditions.

Compassionate Care

Personalized treatment plans, clear pre-op patient education, and continuous post-procedure recovery support.
