Chronic Pain Care Pathway

A structured four-step clinical framework to diagnose nerve targets, apply blocks, and restore function.

1

1. Neurological Assessment

Comprehensive nerve mapping, reflex testing, and radiological MRI review to locate nerve impingements.

2

2. Diagnostic Nerve Block

Targeted injection of local anesthetic around specific nerve plexuses to confirm exact pain pathways.

3

3. Multidisciplinary Program

Combining nerve blocks, physical therapy, non-opioid nerve modulators, and ergonomic adjustments.

4

4.Journal Tracking

Digital pain scale VAS tracking and long-term relief monitoring inside the ApexPain patient portal.
