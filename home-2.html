Spine & Nerve Specialties

Specialty Pillars

Sub-Specialty Pain Disciplines

Targeted minimally invasive procedural care tailored by board-certified anesthesiologists.

Interventional Spine Procedures

Fluoroscopy-guided cervical, thoracic, and lumbar epidural steroid injections, transforaminal nerve blocks, and facet joint injections for disc herniations and stenosis.

Diagnostic Nerve Blocks & RFA

Selective nerve root blocks, stellate ganglion blocks, occipital nerve therapy, and thermal radiofrequency ablation (RFA) providing 6-12 months of sustained relief.

Neuromodulation & Anesthesiology

Spinal cord stimulation (SCS) trials, intrathecal pump management, and sub-specialty conscious sedation protocol for anxious or sensitive patients.

Precision Safety

Fluoroscopic Precision & Anesthetic Comfort

Every interventional procedure at ApexPain is performed in our AAAHC-accredited surgical suite under real-time high-definition C-arm fluoroscopy with radiopaque contrast verification.

Sub-Millimeter Targeting

Continuous live image guidance ensures exact delivery of anti-inflammatory medication directly to inflamed nerve roots.

Monitored Anesthesia Care (MAC)

Attending anesthesiologists deliver gentle intravenous sedation for complete patient comfort throughout procedure steps.

Workflow

Three-Phase Interventional Process

Our structured clinical protocol ensures safety, precision, and measurable outcome success.

1. Diagnostic Imaging Mapping

Review of MRI spinal scans and physical neurological exam to determine exact pain generators.

2. Image-Guided Procedure

Fluoroscopic insertion of therapeutic medication or radiofrequency energy under conscious sedation.

3. Physical Rehab Integration

Transition to active physical therapy and core strengthening during the relief window.
