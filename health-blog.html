Non-Opioid Strategies for Chronic Relief

Modern interventional pain management combines targeted nerve injections with physical therapy and non-addictive nerve modulators, avoiding chronic opioid dependency while achieving superior functional outcomes.

  • Image-guided target precision minimizes medication dosage requirements.
  • Radiofrequency ablation deactivates sensory nerve signaling safely.
  • Physical rehabilitation during relief windows restores core alignment.
