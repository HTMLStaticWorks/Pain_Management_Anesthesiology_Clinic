Patient Journey

Our Clinical Pathway to Pain Relief

A thorough diagnostic and interventional treatment model designed for lasting functional recovery.

Step 1

Initial Intake

Comprehensive pain evaluation, symptom history review, and baseline functional assessment.

Step 2

Diagnostic Imaging

MRI, CT, and fluoroscopy imaging review to pinpoint exact anatomical nerve and disc targets.

Step 3

Anesthesia Review

Pre-anesthetic evaluation ensuring patient safety, cardiac clearance, and optimal sedation parameters.

Step 4

Treatment Design

Tailored intervention strategy combining spinal injections, nerve blocks, and physical therapy.

Step 5

Image-Guided Procedure

Outpatient procedure performed under real-time fluoroscopy or ultrasound guidance for maximum accuracy.

Step 6

Recovery Monitoring

Post-procedure PACU observation by specialized nursing staff to ensure comfortable discharge.

Step 7

Physical Therapy

Targeted rehabilitation exercises to strengthen core and spinal alignment during maximum relief windows.

Step 8

Pain Journal Tracking

Ongoing digital pain rating VAS tracking and follow-up consultations in the ApexPain patient portal.
